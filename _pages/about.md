I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Environmental Engineering at Van Yüzüncü Yıl University, Türkiye. I received my M.Sc. (2011) and Ph.D. (2019) in Environmental Engineering from Atatürk University; my doctoral research examined lipid production by the microalgae *Botryococcus braunii* and *Chlorella zofingiensis* in a bubble column photobioreactor. I joined Van Yüzüncü Yıl University as a research assistant in 2010, became Assistant Professor in 2023 and Associate Professor in 2024.

My research combines **environmental systems modelling** with experimental work: forecasting and trend analysis of **air pollution** with hybrid time-series models, **adsorption and electrochemical processes** for wastewater treatment, and **microalgal cultivation and biotechnology**. I serve on the editorial boards of the *Black Sea Journal of Environmental Sciences* and the *Journal of the Institute of Science and Technology* (Iğdır University), and chair the Bologna Commission of my department.
